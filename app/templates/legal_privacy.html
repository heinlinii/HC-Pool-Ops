Heinlin Field Ops Privacy Policy

Effective July 9, 2026

This policy explains how Heinlin Concrete LLC handles information in Heinlin Field Ops.

Information handled

The service may process names, contact details, property and job information, schedules, field notes, photos, employee time records, device location data used for authorized GPS and stop tracking, billing records, and connected-service data.

QuickBooks Online data

When an authorized administrator connects QuickBooks Online, the service may access customer, invoice, payment, company, and related accounting information needed to show balances, prepare statements, support billing workflows, and perform actions approved by an authorized user. QuickBooks credentials are not displayed to ordinary users. OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored server-side and are used only to maintain the authorized connection.

How information is used

Information is used to operate jobs, schedule work, document field activity, provide customer service, manage billing, improve workflow, protect company records, and perform actions requested by authorized users.

Sharing

Information is shared only with service providers and connected platforms needed to operate the service, with authorized company users or customers, or when required by law. Heinlin Concrete LLC does not sell personal information.

Location and GPS

GPS data is collected only from authorized users and devices when field tracking is enabled. It is used for clock-in, route, stop, job-site, safety, and operational records. Device permissions can be managed in the browser or operating system.

Retention and security

Records are retained as reasonably needed for business, legal, accounting, safety, and support purposes. Administrative, technical, and access controls are used to protect stored information, although no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Access, correction, deletion, and disconnection

Authorized users may request correction or deletion of appropriate records by contacting Heinlin Concrete LLC. QuickBooks access can be revoked through the disconnect page by an authorized administrator, which removes the stored connection tokens from Heinlin Field Ops.

Contact

Privacy questions may be directed to Heinlin Concrete LLC through the contact information published at hc-pools.com.
