Heinlin Field Ops Terms of Use

Effective date: July 10, 2026

Heinlin Field Ops is an internal business operations platform operated by Heinlin Concrete LLC. It supports scheduling, field logs, customer records, billing workflows, GPS-assisted job tracking, photos, and integrations such as QuickBooks Online and Fieldy.

Authorized use

Access is limited to authorized Heinlin Concrete personnel, customers, and invited users. Users must protect their login credentials and use the system only for legitimate business purposes.

Third-party services

QuickBooks, Fieldy, mapping, weather, storage, email, and other connected services are governed by their own terms. Heinlin Field Ops may exchange only the information needed to perform requested business functions.

Accuracy and approvals

Users remain responsible for reviewing invoices, statements, schedules, and other business records before final submission or sending. Automated suggestions are tools and are not legal, tax, or accounting advice.

Availability

The service is provided on an as-available basis. Features may change as the system is improved.

Contact

Questions may be directed to Heinlin Concrete LLC through the contact information shown on hc-pools.com.
