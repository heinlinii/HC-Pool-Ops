Heinlin Field Ops Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 10, 2026

Heinlin Concrete LLC uses Heinlin Field Ops to operate its business and serve customers. This policy explains the information handled by the platform.

Information collected

The system may store names, contact information, service addresses, project records, invoices, payments, field notes, photos, device location points while GPS tracking is active, and recordings or transcripts submitted through Fieldy or voice tools.

How information is used

Information is used to schedule and perform work, communicate with customers, prepare and send billing records, document job progress, improve safety and accountability, and maintain company knowledge.

QuickBooks Online

When connected, Heinlin Field Ops may read customers, invoices, balances, and related accounting data and may create or send approved accounting actions. OAuth credentials are used to maintain the connection and can be revoked by disconnecting QuickBooks.

GPS

GPS data is collected only through enabled tracking features for work-related routing, clock-in verification, stop records, and job documentation. Browser and device permissions control whether location is shared.

Sharing and security

Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate the platform or when required by law. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Retention and requests

Business records are retained as needed for operations, legal obligations, and historical documentation. Requests concerning personal information may be directed to Heinlin Concrete LLC.
